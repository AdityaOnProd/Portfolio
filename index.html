About Me

I am a DevOps Engineer who actually writes code. I didn’t start my career safely in the cloud; I started deep in the backend, untangling complex database queries and building Java microservices. That foundation is my secret weapon. I don't just deploy applications—I know exactly how they are built from the ground up, how they consume memory, and why they break.

My obsession with making things run faster and never crash naturally bridged the gap between code and infrastructure, pulling me into performance engineering and full-scale DevOps. Today, my working philosophy is simple: automate the pain away. Whether I'm architecting AWS environments, building CI/CD pipelines, or experimenting with Agentic AI workflows, my goal is to eliminate manual toil and make deployments bulletproof.

I’m high-energy, intensely curious, and a builder at heart. Whether I’m scaling enterprise architecture for thousands of users or launching my own digital products and brands from scratch, I bring an entrepreneurial intensity to everything I touch. I love solving hard problems, and I have a lot of fun doing it.
